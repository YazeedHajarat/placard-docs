Shopify theme

Placard documentation

Everything you need to set up and run your store with Placard. Every setting mentioned here is in the theme editor: Online Store → Themes → Customize. Need help? Email yazeedmhajarat@gmail.com.

1. Getting started

  1. Add Placard to your store from the Shopify Theme Store, then click Customize.
  2. Choose a style. Placard comes with four presets: Placard (warm and editorial), Gallery, Nocturne (dark) and Verdigris. You can change colors and fonts later in Theme settings.
  3. Upload your logo: Theme settings → Logo and favicon.
  4. Create your menus in Content → Menus (or Online Store → Navigation), then pick them in the Header and Footer.
  5. Set up filters for your collections with the free Search & Discovery app (see Collection pages).
  6. Work through the home page sections from top to bottom. Click a section to edit it, drag to reorder, or use Add section.
  7. Preview on mobile with the phone icon at the top of the editor, then click Publish.

Tip: settings only appear when they apply. For example, the options for the shrinking header only show when the sticky header is on.

3. Header, menus and announcement bar

Header

  • Desktop layout: logo left with menu centered, logo left with menu beside it, logo centered, logo centered with the menu below, or a menu button only.
  • Mega menu: click Add block → Mega menu and type the top-level menu item it belongs to. Choose the style and number of link columns, show collection images, and add a featured product or up to two promotion images. Other menu items with sub-menus show as dropdowns.
  • Highlighted menu item: type the name of a menu item (for example "Sale") to show it in your accent color.
  • Sticky header: always, only when scrolling up, or off. It can shrink and show a shadow while scrolling.
  • Transparent on home page: the header sits over your first slideshow or hero image. Choose light or dark text, and upload an inverse logo in Theme settings.
  • Search: a search icon that opens a full search window with popular searches and suggested products, or a search bar in the header. On mobile you can add a search bar below the header.
  • Account sign-in: shows Shopify's sign-in and account menu. Requires customer accounts: Settings → Customer accounts.
  • Country and language selectors appear when you sell in more than one market or language.
  • Mobile menu: choose the drawer style, size and link size, show social icons and the language selector, and add a promotion image at the bottom.

Announcement bar

Add one block per message. Choose rotating messages or a continuous scrolling marquee, with speed control. On large screens it can also show a small menu (for example Help, Stores), social icons and the country selector.

Media and layout

  • Desktop media layout: stacked, carousel, thumbnails on the left, or a single image. Shoppers can zoom with a hover magnifier, a full-screen lightbox, or both.
  • Only show images of the selected variant: assign an image to each variant in Shopify. The images after a variant's image belong to that variant until the next variant image.
  • Sticky add to cart bar appears when the main button scrolls out of view.
  • Videos, YouTube/Vimeo links and 3D models are supported. 3D models can be viewed in your room on supported phones.
  • Quick add: turn it on in Theme settings → Product cards. Shoppers choose options and add to cart from any product card.

6. Collection pages and filters

Create your filters (once)

  1. Install the free Search & Discovery app by Shopify.
  2. Open it, go to Filters and click Add filter. Good choices: Availability, Price, Product type, Color, Size and Material.
  3. Save. Filters now appear on every collection and on search results.

In the theme editor, a collection without filters shows a short setup guide that only you can see.

Collection settings (Product grid section)

  • Banner: text only, image beside the text, or text over the image, with breadcrumbs, product count and the collection description above or below the products.
  • Sub-collection links: pick a menu to show links (with images) to related collections.
  • Grid: products per page, columns on desktop and mobile, a grid size switch for shoppers, and "Load more", infinite scroll or page numbers.
  • Filter layout: sidebar, top bar or a drawer. Phones and tablets always use the drawer.
  • Quick filter bar: type a filter name (for example Product type) to show its values as one-click buttons above the products.
  • Filter style blocks: add one per filter to show it as a checkbox list, buttons, color swatches or image swatches; open or closed; how many values before "Show more"; or hide it. Drag the blocks to change the filter order.
  • Promotion tile blocks: place an image or message between products, after the product number you choose.

7. Cart

  • Cart type: a drawer beside the page, a small "Added to your cart" popup, or the cart page.
  • Free shipping progress: set the amount; an optional second goal unlocks a free gift.
  • Discount codes: shoppers enter codes in the cart and see them applied immediately.
  • Shipping estimator on the cart page shows rates for a country and postal code.
  • Order note, gift wrap and gift message: for gift wrap, create a gift wrap product and select it.
  • Terms checkbox: checkout is enabled only after shoppers agree.
  • Reserved timer: a calm "your cart is reserved for 10 minutes" message (items are never removed).
  • Undo: when an item is removed, shoppers can undo it.
  • Empty cart: choose a collection to suggest products.

8. Search

Search suggests products, collections, pages and articles as shoppers type. The search results page uses the same filters and sorting as collections. Choose popular searches and suggested products in the Header settings.

9. Blog

  • Blog layouts: editorial (a large lead story), grid, or list, with a journal search, tag filters and "Load more".
  • Blog posts show reading time, a reading progress bar, a table of contents built from your headings, share and copy link buttons, an author box, newer and older posts, and related posts.
  • Author box: uses the staff member's name, photo and bio (Settings → Users).
  • Shop the story: show products under a post. Create a metafield for blog posts in Settings → Custom data → Blog posts with namespace and key custom.products and type Product (list), then pick products on each post. Without it, the products chosen in the section settings are shown.
  • Use headings (Heading 2 and Heading 3) in your posts so the table of contents can be built.

11. Footer

  • Promises: up to 4 short promises with icons (delivery, returns, guarantee, help) across the top.
  • Email signup as a full-width banner or a column.
  • Blocks: brand text and social icons, menus, contact details, text, image with caption, and app blocks. The columns adjust to the blocks you add.
  • On phones, menus fold into tap-to-open sections.
  • Large wordmark: your store name across the full width (solid, outline or subtle).
  • Curtain reveal: on desktop, the page slides up to reveal the footer.
  • Country and language selectors, payment icons, policy links, copyright text and a back to top link.

12. SEO

Placard takes care of the technical side: one main heading per page, page titles with page numbers, canonical links, social sharing previews, structured data for your store, products, breadcrumbs and blog posts, crawlable pagination, fast loading and image alt text that falls back to the product name.

What you should do:

  1. Set your store title, description and social sharing image in Online Store → Preferences.
  2. Fill in Search engine listing (title under 60 characters, description under 160) for products, collections, pages and posts.
  3. Add alt text to images: describe what is in the photo.
  4. Write 2 to 3 sentences for each collection description.

13. Languages and currencies

Placard's storefront text is translated into English, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Dutch. Add languages in Settings → Languages and markets in Settings → Markets; the selectors appear automatically. To change any wording, use Online Store → Themes → … → Edit default theme content.

14. Questions and troubleshooting

My filters don't show

Create filters in the Search & Discovery app and check that "Enable filtering" is on in the collection's Product grid section. Filters only show values that products in the collection have.

The account icon doesn't open sign-in

Turn on customer accounts in Settings → Customer accounts, and check "Show account sign-in" in the Header.

Color swatches are grey

Set a color or image on each option value in Shopify (product variants or the Search & Discovery app), or use standard color names such as "Black" or "Sage".

Reviews don't appear

Install a reviews app that supports Shopify's product rating, then add the Product rating block. Many review apps also offer their own app block.

The transparent header isn't transparent

It only applies on the home page, and looks best when the first section is a Slideshow or Hero with an image. Check "Transparent on home page" in the Header settings.

Animations feel too busy

Turn off "Reveal sections on scroll" in Theme settings → Shapes and motion. Visitors who prefer reduced motion on their device never see animations.

How do I update Placard?

When a new version is released, the Themes page shows an update notice. Updating creates a copy with your settings carried over; preview it, then publish.
